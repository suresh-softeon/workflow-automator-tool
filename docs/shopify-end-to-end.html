Shopify Orders and DOMS

The whole journey of an order, from the customer's checkout to the parcel on the doorstep

1. The one-minute story

Shopify is your own online shop. The customers are yours and the money comes to you. Behind the shop is your warehouse.

  • A customer pays for an item on your Shopify store.
  • Shopify records the order, but it does not hold your stock or ship anything.
  • So Shopify tells DOMS: "A new order arrived."
  • DOMS creates the order in your systems, the warehouse ships it, and tracking goes back to Shopify so the customer is notified.

2. Why does DOMS pull or receive the order?

QuestionSimple answer
Why doesn't Shopify handle everything?Shopify sells and takes payment. Your stock, warehouse and carriers live in your own systems.
Why create it in DOMS?DOMS holds the order together with those from your other channels, so stock, shipping and reporting run from one place.
Who checks we can ship?Your systems do. Shopify only knows the stock count it was given.
Does Shopify ask us to accept the order?No. The order is confirmed once the customer pays. Unlike Amazon, there is no acknowledgement step.

4. End-to-end flow

5. The flow, step by step

1
Customer checks out.
The payment is taken. Shopify creates the order with a number such as #1042.
2
Shopify alerts DOMS (webhook, e.g. orders/create).
It is like a doorbell. DOMS doesn't have to keep asking. As a safety net, DOMS also asks the Orders API on a schedule, in case an alert was missed.
3
DOMS reads the full order.
Items, quantities, prices, taxes, shipping address, shipping method and customer details.
4
DOMS creates the order and releases it to SAP/WMS.
It stores the Shopify order ID so everything can be matched later. Duplicate alerts are ignored using that ID.
5
The warehouse picks, packs and ships.
Stock is reserved and the parcel is handed to the carrier.
6
DOMS sends the fulfillment back to Shopify.
It carries the carrier name and tracking number. Shopify marks the order Fulfilled and emails the customer.

6. What if the order is cancelled?

With Shopify you control the cancellation. There is no partner to ask, which makes it simpler than Amazon.

SituationWhat to do
Cancelled before shippingCancel the order in Shopify (Admin or API). Choose whether to refund the payment and whether to restock the items.
Part of the order can't shipRefund or remove only those lines, and ship the rest.
Already shippedIt can't be cancelled. Handle it as a return and refund.
Keep both sides in step. If DOMS cancels but Shopify is not updated, the customer still sees an active order and may still be charged. Cancel in Shopify as well, then confirm the order status there.

7. Worked example

  1. Ravi buys a backpack on your store and pays.
  2. Shopify sends DOMS a webhook, and DOMS fetches order #1042.
  3. DOMS creates the order in SAP and the warehouse starts work.
  4. The backpack is packed and handed to the carrier.
  5. DOMS sends the tracking number to Shopify, and Ravi gets a shipping email.

Alternative ending: the stock count was wrong and the backpack isn't available. DOMS cancels the order in Shopify with a refund, and Ravi is told.

8. Good practice for DOMS

  • Verify every webhook before trusting it. Shopify signs each one, and the check uses your secret from a secret store, never from code.
  • Ignore duplicates. Shopify may send the same alert more than once.
  • Keep a scheduled catch-up poll for missed alerts.
  • Store the Shopify order ID and fulfillment ID in DOMS.
  • Watch for API rate limits and retry with a delay.
  • Keep inventory counts in Shopify in step with your warehouse to avoid overselling.
