Amazon VDF vs Shopify

How the two order flows differ for DOMS

1. The core difference in one picture

One line: with Amazon you fulfil an order that Amazon owns and must answer it. With Shopify you fulfil your own store's order and nobody needs to approve it.

2. Side-by-side comparison

3. What it means for DOMS

AreaAmazonShopify
IntakeScheduled polling jobWebhook listener plus a catch-up job
Order state before release"Awaiting acknowledgement" until Amazon confirmsRelease straight away
Extra tracking neededStore every transactionId and its codeStore the Shopify order ID and fulfillment ID
Cancel handlingKeep a "cancel pending at Amazon" state until getOrder confirmsCancel in Shopify, then confirm the order status
Main risksMissing the 24-hour window, rejected acknowledgements, metrics hitsMissed or duplicate webhooks, overselling from stale stock
Common mistake in both: marking the order cancelled in DOMS before the platform confirms it. The customer or Amazon then still sees an active order.

4. What stays the same

  • The platform sells, and you fulfil. Stock, warehouse and carrier stay in your systems.
  • DOMS creates its own order from the platform's order and releases it to SAP/WMS.
  • Tracking is sent back to the platform after shipment.
  • Orders can't be cancelled after they ship.
  • Both need duplicate protection, retries, and logs that never include tokens or personal data.
